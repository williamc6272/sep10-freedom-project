My forensic science project looks at how technology can make crime solving faster, more affordable, and eco friendly. Right now, many tools like DNA analysis systems are expensive and resource heavy, which slows down investigations and limits access for smaller labs.

I built a website to share my research using a navbar to move between sections, an accordion to show pricing, and cards to highlight future innovations. I also used a frame to design a model of a quantum computer to visualize how advanced computing could speed up forensic analysis and use less resources.

My goal was to create technology that uses fewer resources while improving accuracy and efficiency. This project shows how innovation can make forensic science more accessible and effective.
